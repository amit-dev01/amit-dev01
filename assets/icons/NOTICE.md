Notes:

- Brand SVG paths are recoloured to each project's brand colour. **Apache Tomcat's** official
  `#F8DC75` is darkened to `#B8860B` so it stays legible on light backgrounds.
- `spring-data-jpa` uses the **Spring** leaf, as Spring Data JPA has no mark of its own.
- **Mockito** and **Lombok** have no open-licensed brand mark, so they use neutral Lucide
  glyphs (a test tube and a wand) tinted to the profile's accent colour. They are

  deliberately *not* faked brand logos.
